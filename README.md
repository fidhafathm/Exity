# Exity

What we’re making:

You know how in normal PE, once you invest your money, it’s stuck for like 10 years? You literally can’t get it out or sell your share to someone else. We’re fixing that.

When someone invests in a PE fund through our platform, they get tokens (like digital certificates) that prove they own X% of the fund.
The magic part? They can sell those tokens to other people anytime they want. For USDC. Instantly. On Solana.

So instead of money being locked for a decade, investors have an exit whenever they need it.

Whats the difference with already existing secondary markets?

Secondary PE markets exist but are slow, expensive, and fragmented. We're bringing instant liquidity to PE using Solana's speed and stablecoin rails - turning week-long settlements into millisecond trades.
